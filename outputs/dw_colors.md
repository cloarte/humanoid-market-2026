# Datawrapper colours

Paste these hex codes in each chart (Refine > Colors).

- background: `#F4F1EA`
- text: `#2B2B2B`
- United States: `#33506B`
- China: `#C9903A`
- Other: `#8A7CA3`
- secondary text: `#7B7A77`
- gridline: `#D6D3CD`

Background and fonts come from the Datawrapper theme (custom themes are a paid add-on).
